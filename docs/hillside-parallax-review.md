# Hillside parallax preflight

Baseline and public Pages `19df4a68870bc5c87c07c1d9500ca714a08768c9`. Chromium at 740×360, seed 33, with the real camera and physics stepping. Over a Caves descent from about 5,613 to 5,648 m, the player dropped 271 world px and the camera moved 252 px. The same far limestone scree and near layered shelf stayed at 263.7 and 285.6 screen px. On a shallower Forest run the same near root moved 319.6 to 314.0 px solely from the old `.42` camera term.

`index.html` builds the collision/foreground curve in `makeTerrain.surfaceAt` and tracks `terrain.anchorAt(player.x)` in scene `update`. The midground renderers use screen-space images with scroll factor zero. `ForestMidground.drawBand` and `BiomeMidground.formation` place roots and banks on independent synthetic height profiles and subtract `clamp(cameraY * rate, ±13% of viewport height)`. It saturates on the route's absolute altitude. The existing gap-rim mask uses the true motion, which exaggerates separation from the screen-locked support.

For both 16% and 42% bands, map each fixed placement to a fixed world terrain coordinate. Read the existing Hermite curve and use a continuous visual span between true bank lips across gaps. Smooth and bound *local* relief around the camera's ground reference, then separately project camera lag at the band's normal rate. Project the painted contact, bank, ceiling and arch footings together. Keep existing texture offsets, silhouette scale, depths, biome weights, masks and cache budgets. Clamp sampling to the generated terrain and the correct side of the portal. Rendering may read the terrain and may never extend or mutate it. Use a small shared per-frame interpolation cache without new asset loads. Existing foreground and abyss occlusion own the visible contact with gameplay.

Validate compound hills and flat contact, camera-only movement, rewind/rotation and slopes near gaps, both renderers across the six fades and portal/Boss, and asset fallback and disposal. Compare motion captures and matched gameplay, terrain generation and RNG against the baseline. Verify public bytes and the actual live hillside after release.
